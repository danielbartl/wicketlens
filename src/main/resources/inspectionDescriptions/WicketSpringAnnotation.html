Reports possible annotation and manual-injection mistakes in Wicket Spring usage.

Resolved @SpringBean expresses local injection intent. Jakarta @Inject and @Autowired checks additionally require recognized Wicket Spring registration in the same module and production/test source set.

Wicket's injector cannot inject static or primitive fields. Its construction listener automatically injects Components and Behaviors; models, sessions, and helpers need an explicit injection path. The listener does not process @Autowired.

Recognized manual Wicket injection suppresses missing-path findings. Resolved Spring autowiring APIs and management annotations/factories suppress the Autowired finding. These are bounded intent checks, not proof that every instance is injected. Unknown ancestry or exceeded search bounds suppress missing-path findings.

Checks use source hierarchy and callers in the same module/source set. Custom external factories and lifecycle arrangements may need review or normal inspection suppression. No automatic annotation replacement or constructor edit is offered.
