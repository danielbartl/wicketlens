Reports possible serialization or proxy problems in Wicket Spring injection fields.

Transient injected fields may lose their values after ordinary page deserialization. Local custom serialization hooks make that conclusion uncertain and suppress the warning.

Proxy-specific findings additionally require one recognized direct injector registration in a single source application in the same module/source set. With wrapping enabled, final concrete field types cannot be subclass-proxied. With wrapping disabled, final non-Serializable field types may prevent page serialization. String, primitive-wrapper, and enum fields retain Wicket's direct-value exceptions.

Unknown, indirect, conditional, or ambiguous setup does not imply a proxy policy. Bean lookup and runtime profiles are not resolved. No automatic rewrite is offered.

Configure severity or disable this inspection under Editor | Inspections | WicketLens.
